Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

What's Collected

Inventle collects anonymous gameplay statistics to improve the game. This includes:

  • Number of guesses per puzzle
  • Bonus round results
  • Approximate country (derived from IP address)
  • General device type (mobile/desktop)

What's Not Collected

  • No IP addresses stored — ever
  • No names, emails, or account information
  • No precise location data
  • No personal identifiers of any kind
  • No device fingerprinting or cross-session tracking
  • No cookies for tracking

IP Addresses & Identifying Data

Inventle never stores IP addresses. When gameplay stats are submitted, a third-party geolocation service determines your approximate country from your IP. The IP is processed in-transit only and is never logged, saved, or recorded by Inventle in any form.

Local Storage

Your game progress, statistics, and preferences are stored locally on your device using browser localStorage. This data never leaves your device.

Advertising

Inventle may display advertisements through third-party ad networks (such as Google AdSense). These networks may use cookies to serve ads based on your browsing activity. You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings.

Data Sharing

Inventle does not sell, trade, or share any collected data with third parties, except as required by ad network integrations.

Children's Privacy

Inventle does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach out at inventle.game@gmail.com
